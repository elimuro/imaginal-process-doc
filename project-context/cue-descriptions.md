## Improvisational Cues

### Dialogue of Dissonance

Experiment with dissonant, clashing notes or sounds. Embrace tension and explore how it resolves or transforms. Use contrasting pitches, rhythms, or dynamics to create a sense of conflict or conversation.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 277-15034](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=277-15034)

### Surprise Accent

Play a sudden, loud note or phrase as a surprising accent within a quieter passage. Use this sparingly to create a moment of tension or excitement.

- Status: Complete
- Tags: Effect
- Figma: [node 254-20619](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=254-20619)

### Crossfade

Transition from one sound or technique to another smoothly, blending the two together. Work with another musician to create overlapping sounds that blend seamlessly.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 252-5844](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=252-5844)

### Decay and Fade

Gradually reduce the volume and intensity of your sound, letting it decay naturally. Imagine the sound fading into the distance, becoming softer and more delicate.

- Status: Complete
- Tags: Effect
- Figma: [node 276-13357](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=276-13357)

### Sound Painting

Use your instrument to create a sound that evokes an image, feeling, or scene (e.g., ocean waves, wind, a heartbeat). Focus on the mood or story behind the sound, rather than playing traditional notes or rhythms.

> Note: This is a different type of cue. The direction of these cues is written and unique across instances and doesn't fit well into our framework. Need an alternate way to communicate this since it can't be symbolized effectively.

- Status: Cancelled

### Thematic Variation

Take a simple motif or theme and explore variations by altering rhythm, pitch, or dynamics. Think of it as a creative re-interpretation of a familiar idea.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 277-14915](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=277-14915)

### Call to Silence

Signal a moment of collective silence. Let the entire ensemble pause and hold the space quietly. Use this as a reset point or a moment of reflection before continuing.

> Note: This is a macro-level cue that doesn't take the place of an improvisational cue but overrides all cues for a given player — when it is triggered, all other cues for that player are halted. We can illustrate this by halting all cues or by substituting all cues with some spacious placeholder.

- Status: In Progress
- Tags: Macro

### Timbre Exploration

Focus on changing the quality or color of your sound. Use different techniques to produce unique timbres (e.g., sul ponticello on violin, scraping the gong). Explore contrasting sounds: bright vs. dark, metallic vs. warm.

> Note: Use effects loop here — pixelate, blur, etc.

- Status: Complete
- Tags: Effect

### Pulse and Groove

Establish a steady pulse or groove that others can join in or play against. Focus on creating a rhythmic foundation that anchors the improvisation.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 254-20495](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=254-20495)
- Asset: `Pulse_and_Groove.gif`

### Dynamic Swell

Begin softly and gradually increase in volume (crescendo), then fade back down (decrescendo). Think of it as creating a wave of sound, swelling and receding smoothly. Use techniques like sliding (glissando) or changing bow pressure on string instruments.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 252-1686](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=252-1686)

### Silence and Pause

Embrace moments of silence. Use pauses intentionally to create space and tension. Listen deeply to the soundscape and choose when to play and when to rest.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 277-14815](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=277-14815)

### Layering

Build up layers of sound by adding your part gradually, fitting into the existing texture. Start with sparse notes and increase density as the improvisation progresses.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 213-5002](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=213-5002)

### Drones and Sustained Tones

Hold a long, continuous note or sound, providing a base for others to improvise over. Focus on creating a steady, resonant texture that can evolve slowly.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 253-19989](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=253-19989)

### Echo

Repeat the phrase played by another musician, but slightly softer or with a variation. Create a sense of space and distance, like an echo fading away.

- Status: In Progress
- Tags: Effect

### Call and Response

One musician plays a phrase, and another responds with a contrasting or complementary phrase. Emphasize a conversational dialogue, mimicking a question and answer.

> Note: This is a macro-level cue, meaning it affects all players at once during certain time segments.

- Status: Not Started
- Tags: Macro

## Rhythm Cues

### Flowing Rhythm (Legato)

A smooth, connected rhythm with no sharp breaks or pauses. The notes flow seamlessly into each other.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 214-6732](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=214-6732)
- Assets: `01_RTYM_legato.gif`, `07_RYTM_Flowing.gif`

### Free Rhythm

A flexible, unstructured rhythm where timing is fluid. There's no strict pulse, allowing for expressive phrasing. Ideal for creating a flowing, conversational style of improvisation.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 213-5307](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=213-5307)
- Asset: `02_RTYM_Free.gif`

### Rhythmic Freedom

Play with an irregular, freeform rhythm. Emphasize spontaneity and unpredictability. Try shifting between fast and slow passages or using syncopation for a playful feel.

> Note: Switch to rhythm. Reference: "same as free rhythm?" — shares the `02_RTYM_Free.gif` asset with Free Rhythm.

- Status: Complete
- Asset: `02_RTYM_Free.gif`

### Cyclic Rhythm

A repeating rhythmic cycle that loops continuously. The cycle can be any length (e.g., 7-beat or 12-beat cycle). Helps build a sense of ritual or trance-like continuity.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 234-6945](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=234-6945)
- Asset: `03_RTYM_Cycle.gif`

### Drone with Subtle Pulse

A sustained drone sound with a very subtle, underlying pulse or beat that's almost imperceptible. Creates a meditative, hypnotic feel, where the rhythm is implied rather than explicit.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 234-7304](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=234-7304)
- Asset: `04_RTYM_Drone_and_Pulse.gif`

### Silence and Space

Emphasize the use of rests and pauses, creating rhythm through the absence of sound. Adds tension, anticipation, and can make the next sound feel more impactful.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 234-7348](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=234-7348)
- Asset: `05_RYTM_silence_and_space.gif`

### Call-and-Response Rhythm

One musician plays a rhythmic phrase (the call), and another responds with a complementary or contrasting phrase (the response). Mimics a conversational exchange, adding a playful and interactive element.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 234-7581](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=234-7581)
- Asset: `06_RYTM_Call_and_Response.gif`

### Poly Rhythm

Two or more different rhythms played simultaneously, creating a layered, complex texture. Encourages interaction between musicians, with each playing a distinct rhythmic pattern.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 234-8083](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=234-8083)
- Asset: `08_RYTM_Poly.gif`

### Steady Pulse

Regular, consistent beat like a metronome. This creates a strong foundation and can help ground the improvisation. Useful for establishing a groove or creating a meditative, repetitive feel.

- Status: Complete
- Figma: [node 234-7701](https://www.figma.com/design/b1A4QLSb9tmb6L361Ip6tL/DSP---Visual-Design?node-id=234-7701)
